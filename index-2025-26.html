How to read this

The 2025-26 NBA season as a film. One card per franchise in order of record, then one card per leading scorer, then the season's charts. Every card deep-links: copy the address bar.

  • Team cards — the curve is the season arc: games above or below .500 after every game, so a good season climbs and a bad one sinks. Ticks beneath are the individual results.
  • Player cards — the bars are points scored in each game, in order, with the season average as a line. Players are ordered by total points scored, so availability counts as well as scoring.
  • The NBA Cup Championship does not count toward a team's record, so it sits outside the W-L but is marked on the card.

Credits & sources

Built by Carlo De Marchis — A Guy With A Scarf. A showcase piece, not a commercial product.
