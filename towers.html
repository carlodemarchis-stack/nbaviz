How to read this

The playoff race, game by game. Each bar is a team's wins; the stripes behind it run on to the most it can still reach. All bars share one 82-game scale, so they are comparable across the page and never rescale.

  • Two lines per conference, because the NBA has two cuts: seeds one to six go straight to the playoffs, seven to ten go to the play-in. The dashed marks in the bars sit at the wins of the sixth and tenth teams.
  • Teams are ranked by win percentage, as the NBA ranks them — so a team with games in hand can sit above a longer bar. Hover a team for how many games it is ahead of or behind each line.
  • A team fades once ten teams in its conference already have more wins than it can still reach — it cannot make the play-in, whatever the tiebreaks. A tag fills in once a place is clinched on the same arithmetic.
  • Tags and lines appear once every team has played. On the final night the order is the official one, tiebreakers included.
  • Games lays out every game of every team, one row each: a green box for a win, red for a loss, the opponent's letters inside. Hover a game for the score.
  • Bracket shows how the playoffs went. The NBA Cup Championship does not count toward a team's record, so it is not in the race.
