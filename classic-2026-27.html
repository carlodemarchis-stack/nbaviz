How to read this

The 2026-27 NBA season as a film. One card per franchise in order of record, then one card per leading scorer, then the season's charts. Every card deep-links: copy the address bar.

  • Team cards — the curve is the season arc: games above or below .500 after every game, so a good season climbs and a bad one sinks. Ticks beneath are the individual results.
  • Player cards — the bars are points scored in each game, in order. Green is a night at or above his own season average, red is below it, and gold is his best night of the year. The dashed line is the average itself. Players are ordered by total points scored, so availability counts as well as scoring.
  • The shot chart bins every shot he took into 1.6ft cells: the dot is bigger where he shot more often, and warmer where more of them went in.
  • The NBA Cup Championship does not count toward a team's record, so it sits outside the W-L but is marked on the card.

Credits & sources

Built by Carlo De Marchis — A Guy With A Scarf. A showcase piece, not a commercial product.

  • Schedule, results, box scores, player lines — ESPN's public API. Records reconciled against the official standings for all 30 teams, and every game's box score reconciled against its final score.
  • Crests and portraits — official NBA artwork from cdn.nba.com. Player names use the league's own spelling, accents included.
  • Players are ranked by total points scored in the regular season. Team order is by record.
